## How to read the relations

- Five Lot 51 projects declare Core Library minimums. The publisher shows those project releases alongside its 1.129.39 compatibility labels; this pack does not substitute an independent test, check current availability, or treat the Core Library build as the subject mod version.
- Suite Life names a one-of venue requirement. It is one unresolved alternative relation, not two unconditional requirements. The separate Vacation World condition is retained without modeling automatic dependency selection.
- Healthcare Redux and Get to Work Health Overhaul are compatible according to the author, with GTW core only when both are installed. Their addon modules are not interchangeable. Healthcare Redux's Death Disabler is an optional package, not a separate required project.
- Utility Mods is one publication page with distinct Shared Utilities and Shared Tuning downloads. General Pie Menus belong to Shared Utilities; a relation to the page does not include other files. The author-linked Mood Pack [public post](https://www.patreon.com/lumpinou/posts/mod-lumpinous-43759541) matches the numeric reference from Healthcare Redux's documentation; no download was attempted.
- Education Overhaul's authored Discover University gate applies only to the Education Career module. The same page simultaneously identifies pieces as required and says outside mods beyond its ZIP are not needed. Packaging was not inspected; all three general component statements remain candidates, not independent dependency requirements.
- Family and Friends Activities labels Seasons as recommended. Individual activity gates do not turn it into a full-product requirement.
- Unknown edition/build/package/version and unlisted relationships are kept unknown, not none. The v1 relations projection can show exact-scope Lot 51 evidence, but cannot treat unversioned game pages as accepted version-scoped evidence. No game launch, compatibility test, installation, downloads, paid access, or release publication.
